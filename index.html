Buildtober

31 days. 31 things worth building.

A free build-along for October. Pick a level, roll an idea that solves a real problem, ship it, and stick a sticker on your sheet for every day you build. Made for anyone who wants to skill up by making things in public.

Pick a level

Each idea names the problem it solves and gives you a first step, so you can start in minutes. Not sure? Start with Level 1.

Your sticker sheet

Pin ideas to days, tap a day to log it and collect a sticker when you ship. Your progress stays in this browser.

Starter kit

Your first repo in ten minutes

  1. Create a public GitHub repository called buildtober.
  2. Add one folder per day: day-01, day-02 and so on.
  3. Give each folder a README using the template.
  4. Commit as you go and push before you post.
  5. Add a licence. MIT is a simple, permissive choice.

Small and finished beats big and abandoned. Cut scope, not days.

How the month works

The three levels

Level 1: Quick win.
An hour or two. One clear job: a single script, page or command-line tool.
Level 2: Solid build.
An evening. A small app or tool with tests and a proper README.
Level 3: Showcase.
A weekend. Several parts, docs and a demo. Worth a proper write-up.

Weekly themes

Optional. Press a button to roll ideas in that theme.

  • Days 1 to 7: Threat intelligence and quick wins
  • Days 8 to 14: Open-source AI
  • Days 15 to 21: Detection, cloud and edge
  • Days 22 to 28: Governance, risk and people
  • Days 29 to 31: Polish, share and wrap up

A good build day

  • 5 minutes. Roll an idea. Decide the smallest version that still works.
  • Most of your time. Build. Commit early and often.
  • 20 minutes. Write the README, grab a screenshot, push.
  • 15 minutes. Share it and reply to comments. Others learn from what you made.
